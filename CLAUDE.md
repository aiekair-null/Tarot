# Veyra project rules

- Every visual (videos, promos, cards, screens) follows VISUAL_BIBLE.md. Shared code: veyra_look.py (palette, pixel text, sky, the detailed original Veyra, finishing pass).
- Veyra is the original art.py sprite with more detail; never redesign her.
- Music for every video is video.music_bed (Jynweythek-style piano, new every run). Her voice is on by default.
- Public videos say "contact Veyra"; people's questions, readings and feedback never appear in them.
